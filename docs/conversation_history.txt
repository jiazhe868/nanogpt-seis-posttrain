### 1. Comparative Analysis: PPO vs. DPO vs. GRPO vs. DAPO

| Feature / Metric | **PPO** | **DPO** | **GRPO (Chosen 🏆)** | **DAPO** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Online Actor-Critic | Offline Preference Ratio | Online Group Relative Advantage | Decoupled Online Advantage |
| **VRAM Memory Footprint** | **4 Models** ($\pi_\theta, V_\phi, \pi_{\text{ref}}, R_\psi$) | **2 Models** ($\pi_\theta, \pi_{\text{ref}}$) | **2 Models** ($\pi_\theta, \pi_{\text{ref}}$) | **2 Models** ($\pi_\theta, \pi_{\text{ref}}$) |
| **Value / Critic Model** | **Required** ($V_\phi$) | None | **None** (Group Mean $\bar{R}$) | **None** (Decoupled Mean) |
| **Online Exploration** | Yes | **No** (Offline dataset only) | **Yes** ($G=8$ rollouts) | **Yes** ($G=8$ rollouts) |
| **Rule-Based Verifiers** | Hard to integrate | Impossible (Pairwise loss) | **Native & Seamless** | Native |
| **Memory on 2× A30 (48GB)** | High Overhead | Extremely Low (~1 GB) | **Extremely Low (~1.5 GB)** | Low (~1.5 GB) |
| **Suitability for Math/Science** | Moderate | Poor | **Exceptional** | Good |

### 1. The 3 Major Flaws of Naive Phase 1 Target Extraction

#### ❌ Flaw 1: Prompt Number Pollution & Reward Hacking
- **The Problem:** In Phase 1, we extracted *all* floating-point numbers from the response as `target_numbers`. 
- **The Failure Mode:** If a prompt asks: *"Given rupture area $A = 400\text{ km}^2$, slip $d = 1.5\text{ m}$, and shear modulus $\mu = 30\text{ GPa}$, calculate $M_0$."*
  - Ground truth numbers: `[400.0, 1.5, 30.0, 1.8e19]`.
  - Under naive matching, a model that simply regurgitates: *"Given 400, 1.5, and 30..."* matches 3 out of 4 numbers (75% match ratio) and receives a **$+0.5$ reward**, even if its final calculated answer is completely wrong or missing!
- **Result:** The model learns to copy prompt parameters rather than execute actual multiplication!

#### ❌ Flaw 2: Zero Truth Reward for 65% of the Dataset (Conceptual QA)
- **The Problem:** Over 65% of our SFT dataset consists of `conceptual` and `summarization` questions (e.g., *"Explain why subduction zone beachballs show thrust faulting"*).
- **The Failure Mode:** For conceptual tasks, `target_numbers` is empty `[]`, resulting in $R_{\text{num}} = 0.0$.
- **Result:** The model receives rewards **only for format (`+0.5`) and clean language (`+0.2`)**, but **zero reward for scientific accuracy or factual correctness**. It learns to output beautiful ChatML formatting filled with plausible-sounding gibberish!

#### ❌ Flaw 3: Scientific Notation & Unit Expression Breakdown
- **The Problem:** In seismology, answers are written as `1.8 × 10^19 N·m` or `18.0 E+18`. 
- **The Failure Mode:** Standard float regex
